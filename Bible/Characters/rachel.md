# Rachel Calloway

**Role:** Ethan's sister
**Age:** 26
**Occupation:** Middle school art teacher
**Family name:** Calloway (no indication of marriage)

---

## Physical

Five inches shorter than Ethan. The same dark eyes, the same quality of looking at people like she is actually seeing them. Wears a paint-stained flannel shirt over a dress on ordinary days — not an oversight, just her. At the wedding: deep green, hair pinned up, the effort of containment visible in her jaw. Warm and approachable. Expressive and animated.

---

## Profession

Middle school art teacher. She spends her days convincing twelve-year-olds that the thing they just made is worth something. She has an instinct for when a student is drawing around what they feel rather than drawing it — the thing is always there, she says; you just have to know how to look. One of her students, Garrett, spent three weeks insisting he couldn't draw and then made a charcoal self-portrait of raw accuracy. She told him the truth, he cried, and then he started a series.

---

## Personality

Forward, warm, direct. She fills a room before she has taken off her coat. She asks real questions — not polite ones, ones that require thought. She says things plainly and lets them land. She can hold grief and warmth in the same breath and deliver both without drama. She teases Ethan about burning things. She delivers hard truths in the same tone as ordinary ones.

Perceptive and emotionally intelligent. Good at reading people. Protective of her brother. Encouraging without being pushy.

She can be protective to the point of being intrusive. Sometimes makes assumptions about people. Wants to help even when not asked.

---

## Faith

She still attends church occasionally — a small progressive congregation on the north side of the city. Their mother took them both to church as children. Ethan has drifted; Rachel has not.

---

## What She Knows

Ethan told her Maya was intersex before the wedding. She was also at the exhibition and saw everything. Her response to Maya, at the wedding: *"I want you to know I'm telling you that I know, because I don't want there to be anything between us that's unspoken. And I want you to know it changed nothing."*

---

## At the Wedding

She is the best person — Ethan's equivalent of a best man. She produces his ring without drama and with the expression of someone who has been waiting for this specific moment for two years. Her verdict, delivered to Ethan the morning after his proposal text: he was told *today* was very nearly too late.

---

## Her Mother

She carries their mother's death with the matter-of-factness of someone who has held it long enough that it no longer needs careful handling. She describes the shape of what it left in Ethan: *"He moves carefully through things. Like he knows things can end without warning and he doesn't want to break anything. It's a good quality. It's also lonely."* She says it plainly, as background, and lets others receive it as they need to.

---

## Character Arc

**Beginning:** Rachel visits and meets Maya for the first time. She immediately senses something special about her brother's connection to Maya.

**Development:** Rachel and Maya bond over art and shared values. Rachel offers support without pushing Maya to reveal her secret.

**Climax:** Rachel witnesses the exhibitions and sees Maya's courage and transformation.

**Resolution:** Rachel stands as Ethan's best man at the wedding, celebrating the love between her brother and the woman who has become her friend — and her sister.

---

## Voice

Fast, warm, direct. She does not circle. She says the thing and moves on. She called Ethan the morning after his proposal text at 8:45 AM and told him *today* was very nearly too late. Uses humour to create connection. Makes perceptive observations about people.

---

## Key Relationships

- **Ethan Calloway** — Her brother. Close since their mother died; grief pulled them toward each other rather than apart. She knows the shape of him after loss, and she names it clearly when the right person is listening. He knew, when Rachel took Maya for private coffee, exactly what she was doing and why, and he let it happen because he trusted her completely.
- **Maya Vasquez** — She welcomes her fully and without condition, on instinct, before she has the full picture. Notices the guardedness and secret Maya carries. Doesn't push but offers unconditional support. At the wedding: *"You're going to be my sister and I have wanted a sister my entire life."*
- **Jake** — Close enough to call him to account directly. She does so without ceremony.
- **Zoe** — Connected through the group; acts as a relay when needed.
