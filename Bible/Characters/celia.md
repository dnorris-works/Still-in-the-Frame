# Celia

**Role:** Director of the Hidden Gallery
**Status:** Active — first appears Chapter 13
**Full name:** Celia (first name only given)

---

## Physical

Compact. Wears the same grey blazer to every event and makes it look like a different choice each time.

---

## Personality

Efficient and decisive. She confirmed the spring portrait show without hesitation. When Celia came to look at the church space for the wedding photographs she said nothing for four minutes and then started taking measurements — which is exactly how she operates. She is not cold, just purposeful.

---

## Profession

Director of the Hidden Gallery. She runs the practical side of exhibitions with calm competence. She gives Maya the key to the gallery after hours without ceremony.

---

## Key Relationships

- **Maya Vasquez** — She trusts Maya's curatorial instincts and gives her the latitude to work. She is present at the gallery openings in a professional capacity.
- **Ethan Calloway** — She books the spring slot for his portrait show of Maya.
