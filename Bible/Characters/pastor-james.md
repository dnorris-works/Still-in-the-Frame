# Pastor James

**Role:** Pastor at Riverside Community Church / co-officiant at the wedding
**Status:** Active — first appears Chapter 21
**Full name:** James (first name only given)
**Age:** ~Mid-forties

---

## Physical

Mid-forties. Compact. Unhurried. He has the quality of someone who does not perform authority because he does not need to.

---

## Personality

He opens without ceremony. He talks about things in the practical register — not the grand cosmological version, the working one. His sermons are grounded. Progressive, affirming, theologically thoughtful. Warm and welcoming. Speaks with spiritual wisdom.

---

## The Sermon (Chapter 21)

He speaks about making — the way a handmade thing carries the intention of the maker, the way you can see in brushwork or in joinery the choices that shaped it. *God made us with intention. Not template. Intention.* He says: *God does not make mistakes. God makes people. And people are not mistakes.* He says: *You were made. You are not an accident or an error or a problem to be solved.*

This is the sermon Maya hears on her first Sunday inside a church in years. She has read these words in papers at two in the morning. Hearing them said aloud by a person in a room full of people who believe them is different.
