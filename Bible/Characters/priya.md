## Personality

Strong opinions on most things and no patience for anyone who doesn't bring their full self to a conversation. She is fully present from the first minute — she does not warm up slowly. She gestures widely when she is making a point.

Direct and engaged. She goes deep quickly. She laughs readily when someone says exactly the right thing and points at them when they do.
