# Jake

**Role:** Ethan's best friend and coworker
**Status:** Active — first appears Chapter 2
**Full name:** Not given
**Partner:** Priya

---

## Physical

Tall and loud in a way that makes rooms feel smaller. Physically expressive — leans over to read people's screens, signals approval from across a pub, genuinely engaged with his friends.

---

## Occupation

Exhibition preparator at the City Museum of Contemporary Art.

---

## Personality

Warm, loyal, attentive. He listens with his whole face — not performing attention but giving it. He is direct with Ethan in a way that requires care to distinguish from bluntness. He is capable of disguising his own discomfort as concern for others, and he knows it.

He is not malicious. Outgoing and friendly. Casual in voice, uses humour and teasing. Direct but not always wise. He narrates things like a commentator. He says the true thing about people to their faces, including uncomfortable things. He can be very warm and genuine in conversation.

---

## Interior Life

Jake thinks of himself as a protective friend. This instinct is generally well-calibrated and is one of his best qualities. He genuinely cares about Ethan's happiness and supports his relationship with Maya.

## Key Relationships

- **Ethan Calloway** — His closest friend, eight years. He is the first person Ethan tells things to. He noticed the change before he had words for it.
- **Priya** — His partner. Her opinions are extensive and largely correct, which he freely acknowledges.
- **Maya Vasquez** — Warm and supportive toward her, recognizes her positive impact on Ethan.
- **Rachel Calloway** — Ethan's sister, respects her judgment and care for her brother.
