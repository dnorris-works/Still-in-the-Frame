# Dr. Bowen

**Role:** Maya's academic supervisor
**Gender:** She/her
**Full name:** Not given
**Age:** ~50s

---

## Profession

Academic — Maya's PhD supervisor. Has been reading and giving notes on Maya's chapter drafts throughout the thesis process. University professor; Maya's thesis advisor.

---

## Personality

Economical. Unhurried. Supportive, perceptive, professional. Has a specific expression when genuinely interested rather than professionally attentive, and Maya has learned to read the difference. Uses the word *rigorous* the way other supervisors say *extraordinary* — it carries weight because it is rare. A single nod from Bowen is a full endorsement.

Asks the questions that need asking rather than the ones that are easy to answer. Warm but maintains boundaries. Genuinely intellectually engaged.

---

## Key Insight

She is the one who tells Maya about Douglas Reid (theology faculty), who passed the Voss paper around and said it was *doing theology without knowing it.* This connection leads, indirectly, to Nancy Griffin and the church. Bowen makes the introduction offhandedly, as background detail — and it is one of the most significant moments in the story.

In Chapter 16: *"What is this thesis actually arguing? Not the Voss question. Yours."*

At the portrait exhibition: *"This is the thesis. Not the written version — the real one."* Then, immediately, back to logistics — the defence panel, the examiner from Edinburgh. The ability to hold both registers at once.

In Chapter 26: *"And now? What was it actually about?"* — asked with the inflection of someone inquiring about something larger than administrative next steps.
