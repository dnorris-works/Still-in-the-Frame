# Jordan

**Role:** Member of Nancy Griffin's support group at St. Anne's
**Status:** Active — first appears Chapter 22
**Full name:** Jordan (first name only given)

---

## Physical

About thirty. Taller than Maya realised at first meeting. Has a quality of deliberate stillness — the kind that comes from having learned, the hard way, to take up exactly the space needed and no more.

---

## Personality

Quiet in the particular way of someone who has measured their words for a long time and has chosen which ones are worth spending. He is not closed — he is precise. He speaks in the group without performance or apology.

---

## His Story

He has had a conversation with his family that went badly and then, six months later, less badly. He tells the group about this plainly, as a fact, not as a wound he is still carrying.

He tells Maya, on her first visit, that he can tell it is her first time from the chair she chose. Without unkindness. And then: *"You were never alone. Not for a single day of it. You just didn't know where we were."*

---

## At the Exhibition

He attends the gallery opening with Diane. He stays until nearly the end.

## At the Wedding

He is present. He is seated with Diane.

---

## Key Relationships

- **Nancy Griffin** — He is a regular in her group and trusts the space she has built.
- **Maya Vasquez** — He recognised her at first sight as someone walking in for the first time and chose to say the true thing.
- **Diane** — They attend the exhibition and wedding together.
