# Rachel Calloway

**Role:** Ethan's sister
**Family name:** Calloway (no indication of marriage)

---

## Physical

Five inches shorter than Ethan. The same dark eyes, the same quality of looking at people like she is actually seeing them. Wears a paint-stained flannel shirt over a dress — not an oversight, just her. Arrives with a bag too large for a weekend.

---

## Profession

Middle school art teacher. She spends her days convincing twelve-year-olds that the thing they just made is worth something. She has an instinct for when a student is drawing around what they feel rather than drawing it — the thing is always there, she says; you just have to know how to look.

---

## Personality

Forward, warm, direct. She fills a room before she has taken off her coat. She asks real questions — not polite ones, ones that require thought. She says things plainly and lets them land without softening them. She can hold grief and warmth in the same breath and deliver both without drama.

She is not performing warmth. She takes both of Maya's hands before she has been in the room for thirty seconds and it is not a gesture — it is just her.

---

## Faith

She still attends church occasionally — a small progressive congregation on the north side of the city. Their mother took them both to church as children. Ethan has drifted; Rachel has not.

---

## Her Mother

She carries their mother's death with the matter-of-factness of someone who has held it long enough that it no longer needs careful handling. She speaks of it plainly, as background, and lets others receive it as they need to.

---

## Voice

Fast, warm, direct. She teases without cruelty. She delivers hard truths in the same tone as ordinary ones, which makes them land harder. She does not circle. She says the thing and moves on.

---

## Key Relationships

- **Ethan Calloway** — Her brother. She knows the shape of him after loss — careful, patient, slightly lonely — and she names it clearly when the right person is listening.
- **Maya Vasquez** — She welcomes her fully and without condition, on instinct, before she knows everything.
- **Jake** — Close enough to call him to account directly. She does so without ceremony.
- **Zoe** — Connected through the group; acts as a relay when needed.
