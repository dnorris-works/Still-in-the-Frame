# Zoe

**Role:** Maya's roommate and best friend
**Age:** 26
**Occupation:** Psychology graduate student
**Status:** Active — present from Chapter 2
**Full name:** Not given
**Partner:** Marcus

---

## Physical

No detailed description given. Warm and approachable. Expressive and animated. Confident in her presence.

---

## Personality

Warm, steady, and direct without being hard. She makes room for people — genuinely, not as a performance. She has the particular skill of knowing when to push and when to stay quiet, and she almost always gets it right. She says the hard thing once and does not repeat it. She knows when to leave a room.

She is also funny. She defended her undergraduate thesis outside, by the recycling bins, after a building evacuation. She owns at least four novelty mugs. She can retrieve a glass of wine from a counter without standing up, with a certain lack of dignity, at exactly the right moment to break the gravity of a scene.

Loyal and protective. Perceptive and emotionally intelligent. Encouraging but realistic. Outgoing and social. Strong boundaries and values.

She can be protective to the point of being controlling. Wants to fix things for Maya. Sometimes speaks before thinking.

---

## Background

Has known Maya's secret for three years. One of the only people Maya trusts completely before Ethan. Her psychology background gives her insight into trauma and shame. She understands the cost of hiding.

Zoe's response when Maya told her was to say nothing for a long moment, then: *"Okay. So. Are you hungry?"* Which was exactly right. Zoe's love is practical and unceremonious and bottomless. She has watched Maya hide and has not pushed — not because she doesn't care, but because she trusts Maya to find her own way out.

---

## Interior Life

Zoe has been watching Maya's relationship patterns for years and knows what they cost her. She has been waiting, without demanding, for a very specific thing. When it arrives she sits down on the kitchen floor. Not dramatically — her legs just decided.

She has been watching Ethan see Maya since the beginning. She says on the kitchen floor, on the night of the engagement: *"I kept waiting for you to let him."* Her crying first when Maya tells her about Ethan is not sentimentality. It is three years of relief.

---

## Character Arc

**Beginning:** Zoe is the loyal friend who knows the secret and supports Maya from the sidelines.

**Development:** As Maya falls in love with Ethan, Zoe encourages her to take the risk of being vulnerable and honest.

**Climax:** When Bret threatens to expose Maya's secret, Zoe stands beside her, protective and supportive.

**Resolution:** At the exhibitions, Zoe stands beside Maya, beaming with pride. She witnesses her best friend's transformation from hiding to visibility.

**End:** Zoe is Maya's maid of honour at the wedding, celebrating the woman her best friend has become.

## Voice

Quiet and direct. When she pushes she does it in plain language, without padding. Her warmth comes through in action as much as in speech. Uses humour to lighten heavy moments. Protective but not controlling.
