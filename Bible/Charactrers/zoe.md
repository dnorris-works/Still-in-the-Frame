## Personality

Warm, steady, and direct without being hard. She makes room for people — genuinely, not as a performance. She has the particular skill of knowing when to push and when to stay quiet, and she almost always gets it right. She says the hard thing once and does not repeat it. She knows when to leave a room.

---

## Interior Life

Zoe has been watching Maya's relationship patterns for years and knows what they cost her. She carries this knowledge without weaponising it — she says what needs saying once, clearly, and then puts it in Maya's hands. She is fiercely certain about what Maya deserves, and she means it.

---

## Voice

Quiet and direct. She doesn't perform patience — she gives it. When she pushes she does it in plain language, without padding. Her warmth comes through in action as much as in speech.

---

## Key Relationships

- **Maya Vasquez** — Her roommate and closest friend. The person Maya always tells.
- **Rachel Calloway** — They are connected; Zoe acts as an information relay between Rachel and the wider group.
