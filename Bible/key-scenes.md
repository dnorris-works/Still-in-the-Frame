## The Providence Seed (Ch 10 → pays off Ch 19)

**In Ch 10**, after Maya explains she lost her faith the night Bret rejected her, Ethan plants a seed without naming himself:
> *"Maybe it wasn't that God let it happen. Maybe God knew you needed something Bret couldn't give you."*

He does not say his own name. He lets it sit. Maya doesn't respond to it directly — she turns it over silently and moves on.

**In Ch 19**, after Nancy tells Maya she is loved by God exactly as she is, Maya arrives at the full realisation on her own: Bret had to fail. The night she lost faith was the night God was already working toward this — toward Ethan, toward Nancy, toward this room. She doesn't say it aloud yet. But she knows it.

This is the emotional climax of Maya's faith arc. It must not be resolved before Ch 19.

## Maya's Medical Condition

**Diagnosis:** Complete Androgen Insensitivity Syndrome (CAIS)
- XY chromosomes; body cannot respond to androgens
- Presents externally female; has a phallus (clitoromegaly) and a vagina
- No uterus, no ovaries, internal undescended testes
- Cannot become pregnant
- Condition is X-linked recessive — inherited through maternal carrier line

**Why this matters to the story:**
- Eileen (Maya's mother) is an X-linked carrier — the gene passed through her
- Eileen was told by doctors when Maya was an infant
- This is the origin of Eileen's silence: guilt, not coldness

## Eileen's Breaking Point

**Character:** Eileen — Maya's mother, X-linked CAIS carrier
**Placement:** Act 3, Chapter between the proposal and the wedding (currently Ch 26 or new Ch between 25 and 26)

### What happens
- Eileen sees Ethan with Maya — not at a dramatic moment, an ordinary one
- The way he looks at her; the way he touches her arm without thinking
- Relief and grief arrive simultaneously: the thing she feared most (that Maya would never be loved completely) did not happen
- She begins speaking and cannot finish: *"I knew. Before you did. They told me when you were three weeks old and I —"*
- She does not complete the sentence. Maya understands.
- Maya takes her hand. That is enough, for now.

### What this is NOT
- Not a full confession with a neat resolution
- Not a speech
- Not an immediate forgiveness scene
- Not placed at the wedding — the wedding absorbs everything; this scene needs its own space before it

### The wedding payoff
- Eileen in the front row; does not look away once
- She cries before Ethan does
- No words between her and Maya about the earlier scene — none needed
- Her presence, staying, not looking away — that is what she can give now

### Why Ethan is the trigger
- Ethan's acceptance of Maya is what breaks Eileen's silence
- She spent twenty-six years fearing her daughter would never be loved like this
- Seeing that she is loved like this dissolves the fear — and without the fear, there is nothing left to protect herself with
- The silence that was built on guilt and fear has no foundation left

**Decision:** Accept novella length OR expand via Eileen's story as a second timeline.

**If novella:**

- Nothing structurally wrong with the story at novella length
