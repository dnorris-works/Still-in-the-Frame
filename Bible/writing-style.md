# Writing Style Guide
## Seen and Still Loved: Finding Love When You're Different

---

### Genre & Tone
Contemporary Literary Romance - Raw, emotionally honest, and unflinching. This story does not soften difficult truths. It sits with discomfort, with shame, with longing, and with the transformative power of being completely seen and loved.

---

### Primary Style Influence

**Colleen Hoover (Strength: 8/10)**
- Raw emotional honesty without apology
- Tackles difficult, sensitive subjects directly
- Characters feel real because their flaws and wounds are real
- Intimacy portrayed authentically, not sanitised
- Internal monologue is sharp, immediate, unguarded
- Short punchy sentences during emotional peaks
- Dialogue that sounds like real people, not polished performances
- The reader feels what the character feels - not told what to feel

---

### Secondary Influences

**Emily Henry (Strength: 5/10)**
- Wit and warmth balanced with emotional depth
- Intellectual characters who are also fully human
- Sharp, observational internal voice
- Art and creativity woven naturally into character identity

**Jojo Moyes (Strength: 4/10)**
- Emotional complexity handled with grace
- Relationships that cost something
- No easy resolutions - growth is earned
- Female interiority treated with seriousness

---

### Key Characteristics

**Voice**
- Close third person limited, alternating between Ethan and Maya POV as noted in outline
- Deep interiority - the reader lives inside the character's head
- Internal monologue is immediate, honest, and unfiltered
- Maya's voice is guarded, precise, layered with subtext
- Ethan's voice is observational, warm, quietly perceptive
- Both voices are distinct - a reader should know whose POV without a heading

**Pacing**
- Varied - slow and sensory in intimate moments, fast and clipped during emotional crisis
- Let scenes breathe when emotion is building
- Short paragraphs and sentence fragments at peak emotional moments
- Long, flowing sentences when characters are settled, observing, or content
- Never rush a revelation

**Dialogue**
- Natural, not polished - characters interrupt, trail off, say the wrong thing
- Subtext matters - what isn't said is often more important than what is
- Avoid on-the-nose dialogue - characters rarely say exactly what they mean
- Witty exchanges between Ethan and Maya in early chapters
- Dialogue becomes more direct and honest as trust deepens

**Intimacy**
- Physical intimacy is portrayed honestly and without euphemism
- Emotional intimacy is given equal weight to physical
- The love scene is explicit but purposeful - it is a scene of revelation and acceptance, not just desire
- Body language and sensory detail carry emotional meaning

**Show, Don't Tell**
- Never write "Maya felt ashamed" - show the shame through behaviour, physical response, internal thought
- Never write "Ethan loved her" - show it through his choices, his attention, his patience
- Emotion lives in the body: tight chest, held breath, the hands that don't know what to do
- Let the reader arrive at the feeling themselves

**Art & Photography as Metaphor**
- Art language woven naturally - not as decoration but as character expression
- Ethan sees the world through a photographer's eye - light, composition, what's hidden and revealed
- Maya sees the world through an art historian's eye - context, narrative, what's been concealed
- Their shared artistic sensibility is the foundation of their connection

---

### Vocabulary & Language

- Contemporary, accessible, intelligent
- Neither dumbed down nor pretentious
- Characters speak like educated young adults - natural contractions, occasional profanity where authentic
- Art and photography terminology used correctly and naturally
- Avoid clichéd romance language (eyes like the ocean, heart racing like a drum)
- Favour precise, unexpected observations over familiar ones

---

### Sentence Structure

- **Varied** - this is essential
- Short sentences for impact: *He stayed.*
- Fragments for interiority: *Not yet. Not like this.*
- Long sentences when a character is processing, observing, spiralling
- Paragraph breaks carry emotional weight - a single line paragraph signals importance

---

### Point of View Rules

- **Ethan chapters:** Third person limited, deep interiority. He observes. He notices what others miss. His POV is warm, patient, precise.
- **Maya chapters:** Third person limited, deep interiority. She guards. She deflects. Her POV is intelligent, layered, often in conflict with itself.
- **No head-hopping** - POV switches happen at scene or chapter breaks only
- **Alternating POV** as specified in the outline scene notes

---

### What This Story Does Not Do

- Does not soften the love scene because of moral objection
- Does not sanitise Bret's cruelty in the flashback
- Does not preach - faith themes emerge through character experience, never through authorial commentary
- Does not resolve things too easily - growth is earned and costs something
- Does not explain Maya's intersex condition in clinical or expository terms - it is revealed naturally through her experience and her words

---

### Tone by Story Phase

**Part I - First Glimpses (Ch 1-2):** Warm, witty, anticipatory. The world feels full of possibility.

**Part II - Deepening Colors (Ch 3-4):** Tender, hopeful, gently building tension. Something is being withheld.

**Part III - Developing Trust (Ch 5-7):** Warmer intimacy balanced with Maya's growing fear. Joy and dread in equal measure.

**Part IV - The Hidden Truth (Ch 8-9):** Tension tightens. The secret becomes a weight. The faith crisis is raw and unresolved.

**Part V - True Colors Revealed (Ch 10-12):** The most emotionally intense section. The revelation, the acceptance, the aftermath. Slow and precise.

**Part VI - Living in Full Color (Ch 13):** Expansive, earned, celebratory but grounded. Joy that has cost something is the only real joy.

---

### Sensitivity Notes

- Maya's intersex identity is handled with dignity and specificity - she is not a metaphor, she is a person
- Her body is not described as shocking or grotesque - it is her body, and it is whole
- The intimate scene is written from a place of acceptance and wonder, not voyeurism
- Bret's cruelty is shown as his failure, not Maya's flaw
- Faith is not mocked or dismissed - it is a genuine part of Maya's journey
