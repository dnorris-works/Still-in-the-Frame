# Zoe - Character Profile

## Basic Information
- **Age:** 26
- **Occupation:** Psychology graduate student
- **Education:** Graduate program in psychology
- **Living Situation:** Roommate and best friend of Maya

## Physical Description
- Warm and approachable
- Expressive and animated
- Confident in her presence

## Personality Traits
- Loyal and protective
- Perceptive and emotionally intelligent
- Encouraging but realistic
- Outgoing and social
- Supportive without being pushy
- Strong boundaries and values

## Background
- Has known Maya's secret for years
- One of the only people Maya trusts completely
- Psychology background gives her insight into trauma and shame
- Understands the cost of hiding

## Strengths
- Unconditional acceptance of Maya
- Ability to listen without judgment
- Encouragement without pressure
- Protective instinct balanced with respect for autonomy
- Emotional intelligence
- Loyalty

## Weaknesses
- Can be protective to the point of being controlling
- Wants to fix things for Maya
- Sometimes speaks before thinking

## Key Relationships

**Maya (Best Friend)**
- Knows Maya's deepest secret
- Only person Maya fully trusts before Ethan
- Protective but encouraging her to take risks
- Celebrates her victories
- Witnesses her transformation

**Ethan**
- Approves of him for Maya
- Becomes part of their support system
- Respects his love for Maya

## Character Arc

**Beginning:** Zoe is the loyal friend who knows the secret and supports Maya from the sidelines.

**Development:** As Maya falls in love with Ethan, Zoe encourages her to take the risk of being vulnerable and honest.

**Climax:** When Bret threatens to expose Maya's secret, Zoe stands beside her, protective and supportive.

**Resolution:** At the second exhibition, Zoe stands beside Maya, beaming with pride. She witnesses her best friend's transformation from hiding to visibility.

**End:** Zoe is Maya's maid of honor at the wedding, celebrating the woman her best friend has become.

## Voice & Dialogue
- Warm and encouraging
- Direct and honest
- Uses humor to lighten heavy moments
- Speaks with genuine care
- Protective but not controlling

## Internal Conflicts
- Wanting to protect Maya vs. respecting her autonomy
- Knowing the secret vs. wanting Maya to tell others
- Loyalty to Maya vs. her own life and relationships

## External Conflicts
- Watching Maya struggle with shame
- Bret's threat to expose the secret
- Supporting Maya through her fear of rejection

## Motivations
- To support Maya's happiness and wholeness
- To see her best friend free from shame
- To witness Maya's courage and transformation
- To be part of her journey to visibility

## Role in the Story
Zoe represents the friend who knows your deepest secret and loves you anyway. She's the bridge between Maya's hidden life and her eventual visibility. She's the witness to Maya's transformation and the celebration of her courage.
