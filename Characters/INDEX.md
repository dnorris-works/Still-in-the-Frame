# Seen and Still Loved - Character Index

Quick reference for all characters in this story.

## Main Characters
- **Maya** - Protagonist, 26, intersex art historian
- **Ethan** - Love interest, 28, photographer and museum designer

## Supporting Characters
- **Zoe** - Maya's best friend, psychology student
- **Rachel** - Ethan's sister, art teacher
- **Bret** - Antagonist, ex-boyfriend
- **Jake** - Ethan's coworker, exhibition preparator
- **Dr. Dana Bowen** - Maya's thesis advisor
- **Pastor James** - Pastor at Riverside Community Church
- **Reverend Nancy Griffin** - Co-pastor at Riverside Community Church
- **Jordan** - Support group member, intersex woman
- **Eileen** - Maya's mother; X-linked CAIS carrier; chose silence out of guilt; breaks open when she sees Ethan with Maya
- **Maya's Father** - Pretends the truth doesn't exist
- **The Hidden Gallery Owners** - Gallery operators, supportive

## Story Summary
Contemporary romance about Maya, an intersex woman hiding her truth, who meets Ethan, a photographer who sees her completely. Their journey from first meeting to wedding, with themes of authenticity, acceptance, and love.

## Key Themes
- Authenticity and being fully seen
- Unconditional love and acceptance
- Overcoming shame and reclaiming identity
- Art as expression and revelation
- Faith and spiritual belonging
