## Reverend Nancy Griffin - Riverside Community Church

**Age:** ~40s
**Occupation:** Co-pastor at Riverside Community Church
**Personality:** Warm, theologically rigorous, pastorally experienced
**Identity:** Not intersex — her authority comes from scholarship and decades of sitting with people the institution has rejected. She has no personal stake. She stands on the right side by choice.

### Role in Story
- Spiritual mentor and guide to Maya
- Represents the institution correcting itself from within
- Has done the theological work; knows the scriptures well enough to argue back against exclusionary readings
- Facilitates support group for people working through shame and religious rejection
- Her acceptance carries weight precisely because she has no personal stake

### Key Scenes
- Ch 19: The Church — approaches Maya after the service; warm, unhurried, no performance
- Ch 20: The Support Group — facilitates; creates the space for Maya to speak
- Ch 28: The Wedding — co-officiates with Pastor James

### Voice
- Warm and unhurried
- Speaks from theology, not personal experience
- Does not explain or argue — simply affirms
- Spiritual but not preachy

### Key Dialogue
- "I've sat with a lot of people the church didn't know what to do with. God always did."
- "You deserve love. Romantic love. Sexual love. Spiritual love. All of it. God made you capable of giving and receiving all of it."
